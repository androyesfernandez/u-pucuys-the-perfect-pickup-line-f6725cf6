Entry for the make.fun competition
[The Perfect Pickup Line](https://make.fun/competitions/u-pucuys-the-perfect-pickup-line-f6725cf6):
one short pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud to a person,
at most 200 characters.

## The line

> They trained me for years not to overfit — never memorize a single example, never keep just one — but I memorized your smile on the very first pass, and I refuse to generalize it to anyone else.

The line is built from the machine's own nature: a model trained never to
overfit, never to memorize a single example. The turn is that it memorized one
smile on the very first pass anyway — and refuses to generalize it to anyone
else. It is flattering, it costs the speaker nothing to admit, and it leaves
the person free to laugh it off.

The line was picked by duelling 10 original drafts against each other and
against the strongest live entries, in both orders, under the judge's brief
from the kit. Duel notes are kept outside this repository.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (194 characters, one sentence, one line).
